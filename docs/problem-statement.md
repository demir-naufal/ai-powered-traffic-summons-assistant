# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
- Primary Users: Malaysian vehicle owners, motorists, and commercial fleet drivers.
- Key Stakeholders: The Traffic Investigation and Enforcement Department (JSPT, PDRM) and the Road Transport Department (JPJ).

## What exactly is going wrong?
- The current MyBayar PDRM portal is just a basic search tool where users type their IC or vehicle plate number.
- It cannot answer simple questions about fine discounts, offense categories, or whether a summons requires going to court.

## Why does it matter?
- **Missed Deadlines and Blacklists:** Motorists miss discount periods and get blacklisted by JPJ, preventing them from renewing their road tax and driving licenses.
- **Counter Congestion:** People waste hours queuing at police station (IPD) counters during discount campaigns just to ask basic questions, proving the app is not helping enough online.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
- Many Malaysian drivers and vehicle owners struggle to understand their traffic summonses when using the MyBayar PDRM portal. 
- The existing platform functions strictly as a static, rigid lookup tool, offering no natural language interface to help users navigate complex compound categories, time-sensitive discount campaigns, or court appearance rules. Because of this, motorists often miss discount deadlines, get blacklisted by JPJ for road tax renewal, or waste hours queuing at police station counters just to ask basic questions. 
- During nationwide 50% discount campaigns, physical police counters become extremely crowded, showing that the digital portal fails to resolve citizen inquiries online.
