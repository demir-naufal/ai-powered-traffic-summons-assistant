# AI-Powered Traffic Summons Assistant for MyBayar PDRM

This project designs an AI-powered conversational assistant to help vehicle owners and motorists in Malaysia to easily check traffic summons details, verify fine discounts, and understand court requirements online.

## Problem statement
Many Malaysian motorists and vehicle owners struggle to understand their traffic summonses when using the MyBayar PDRM portal. The current system only acts as a basic search tool, failing to give clear answers about fine categories, active discount campaigns, or court requirements. Because of this, motorists often miss discount deadlines, get blacklisted by JPJ for road tax renewal, or waste hours queuing at police station counters just to ask basic questions. During nationwide 50% discount campaigns, physical police counters become extremely crowded, showing that the digital portal fails to resolve citizen inquiries online.
